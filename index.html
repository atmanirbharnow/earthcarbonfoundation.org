Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about enrollment, data, and revenue.

What does ECF asset registration include? ▼

Registration provides end-to-end baseline modeling, PDD authoring, VVB verification management, public serialization on our registry, and access to institutional carbon buyers. You also receive formal documentation suitable for BRSR and corporate ESG reporting.

What operational data is required from our end? ▼

We only require your baseline facility data: utility electricity bills, monthly generation logs (e.g., solar inverter reports), or water flow meter readings. Our ingestion engine automates the carbon calculations without demanding dedicated staff time.

Is my commercial solar or water recycling plant eligible? ▼

Yes. Any clean energy project (from 50 kW to multiple MWs), water recycling facility (ZLD/STP/ETP), or organic waste project commissioned within eligible crediting windows qualifies under our programmatic aggregation model.

When and how do we receive carbon credit proceeds? ▼

Credits are serialized upon completed VVB verification cycles. You can choose to retire the credits for your organization's own Net-Zero compliance or monetize them through ECF's institutional carbon liquidity network with proceeds disbursed directly in INR.

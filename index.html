Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding our engagement model.

Why ₹4,999?

The ₹4,999 fee covers the technical evaluation of your operational data, standard emission reduction calculations, and the delivery of a board-ready Environmental Value Snapshot in 3–5 working days.

Is this a proposal?

No. The Snapshot is an independent technical report determining whether your operational assets qualify for carbon credit registration and predicting commercial yield.

Does this guarantee carbon credits?

No advisory can guarantee credits before third-party verification (VVB). The ₹4,999 assessment verifies technical additionality first so you don't waste larger sums on non-eligible projects.

What are Third-Party Actual Costs?

These are official fees paid directly to international carbon registries, accredited auditing entities (VVBs), or testing laboratories during formal project registration.

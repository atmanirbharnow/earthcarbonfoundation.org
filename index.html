Frequently Asked Questions

What does the ₹4,999 engagement fee cover?

It covers the technical review of your asset data, initial emission abatement calculations, additionality checks, and delivery of your custom development roadmap in 3–5 days.

Does this guarantee credit issuance?

Final credit issuance depends on third-party verification (VVB) and registry approval. Our initial engagement establishes technical eligibility so you don't waste capital on non-eligible assets.

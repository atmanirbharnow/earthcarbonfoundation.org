Frequently Asked Questions

What does registration include?

Registration establishes your verified institutional project record, unlocks our automated baseline data engine, and initiates eligibility scoping for carbon asset development and multi-standard registry integration.

How are carbon credits issued?

Credits are issued following rigorous third-party DOE/VVB validation, adherence to approved methodologies (e.g., UNFCCC PoA frameworks), and successful verification of measured emission reductions against established baselines.

What are the exact fees and deliverables?

Standard institutional portfolio onboarding is structured at an accessible tier (₹19,999 intake review fee). Deliverables include a verified Impact & Eligibility Report, structured asset ledger entry, and a tailored carbon credit monetization roadmap.

Can ECF work with historical environmental actions?

Yes. Our data engine evaluates retroactive green interventions across campuses, industrial facilities, and community sites to check vintage eligibility under applicable standards.

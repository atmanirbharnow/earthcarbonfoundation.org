Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about EAOS™ and asset registration.

EAOS™ is Earth Carbon Foundation's proprietary operating framework that allows organizations to identify, quantify, register, and monetise environmental assets created through energy efficiency, renewable power, water recycling, and waste heat recovery.
You submit basic information regarding your installed capacity (e.g., Solar kW, WHR capacity, ZLD water logs). Our automated engine calculates your preliminary carbon mitigation potential and generates a preliminary report.
Carbon credits depend on third-party registry verification (e.g., UCR, CDM, Verra). Our Phase 1 "Data Wash" audit determines additionality and methodology fit before any registry fees are spent.

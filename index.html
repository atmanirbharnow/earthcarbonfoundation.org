Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What does the ₹4,999 engagement fee cover?

A: It covers the technical review of your asset data, initial emission abatement calculations, additionality checks, and delivery of your custom development roadmap in 3–5 working days.

Q: How long does the whole process take?

A: Your roadmap arrives in 3–5 working days. Registry development typically runs 6–18 months depending on methodology and VVB schedules, with issuance following successful verification.

Q: What is the total cost to issuance?

A: You pay our flat advisory fees plus third-party actuals (registry, VVB, audits) billed transparently. Indicative standalone third-party costs range ₹2–8 lakh depending on asset size; bundled programmes share these costs. Your exact figure appears in your roadmap. We charge zero success fees — you keep 100% of carbon revenue.

Q: Who owns the carbon credits?

A: You do. ECF never takes ownership or a revenue share of your credits.

Q: What data do I need to submit?

A: Electricity bills or meter data, system capacity plates, equipment specs, location, and operational history. A simple checklist is sent immediately after engagement.

Q: Which registries do you work with?

A: Pathways aligned with Verra, Gold Standard, I-REC and applicable national mechanisms. The right registry for your asset is selected in your roadmap.

Q: Bundled vs Standalone — which is right for me?

A: Under 500 kW → Bundled Programme (shared registry costs). 500 kW and above → Standalone Registry. We confirm during technical review.

Q: What if my asset is not eligible?

A: You receive a written eligibility report with recommended fixes, so you never waste registry capital. Full refund within 24 hours of payment if review has not begun.

Q: Why not go to the registry directly?

A: Registries require methodology documentation, VVB coordination and legal structuring most asset owners have never done. We bundle that expertise, cutting rejection risk and months of trial-and-error.

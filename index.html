Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the onboarding and engagement process.

Q: Do I need an existing carbon credit project? expand_more
A: No. We begin with your underlying environmental action or physical asset (such as solar rooftop, waste heat recovery, or water recycling) and assess whether it has a suitable development pathway.
Q: What if my asset is not eligible for carbon credits? expand_more
A: The assessment still identifies measurable environmental impact and other potential value pathways (e.g., I-REC, internal ESG reporting, or supply-chain contracts). Carbon-credit eligibility is never assumed blindly.
Q: What does the ₹4,999 engagement fee cover? expand_more
A: It covers the technical evaluation of your asset data, initial emission abatement calculations, additionality checks, and delivery of your custom development roadmap in 3–5 working days.
Q: Who owns the environmental/carbon outcome? expand_more
A: You do. Earth Carbon Foundation operates as an advisory and registry development enabler. ECF never takes ownership or revenue share of your credits.
Q: What is the total cost to issuance? expand_more
A: You pay our transparent advisory fees plus third-party actuals (registry, VVB, validation audits) billed directly or shared within pooled cohorts. Indicative third-party costs range ₹2–8 lakh for standalone projects; pooled cohorts share these costs. We charge zero success commission.
Q: Bundled vs Standalone — which is right for me? expand_more
A: Under 500 kW → Bundled Programme (shared registry and audit costs). 500 kW and above → Standalone Registry pathway. We confirm this during your technical review.

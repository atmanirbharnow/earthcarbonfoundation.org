Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about our engagement model.

Why ₹4,999?

The ₹4,999 fee covers the dedicated technical time required to evaluate your operational data, run standard emission reduction models, and deliver a board-ready Environmental Value Snapshot within 3–5 working days.

Is this a proposal?

No. The Snapshot is an independent technical assessment determining whether your assets are suitable for environmental asset development and what commercial yield you might expect.

Does this guarantee carbon credits?

No reputable advisory can guarantee carbon credits prior to independent third-party audit (VVB) and registry review. The Environmental Value Engagement establishes additionality and methodology fit before you spend larger sums on development.

What are Third-Party Actual Costs?

These are mandatory fees paid directly to external entities such as standard registries, accredited independent auditing firms (VVBs), laboratory testers, or official government bodies during full project execution.
